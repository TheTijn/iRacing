How to play

The game

Instant {{ sp.title.toLowerCase() }}: bet on a race of {{ runners() }} {{ sp.nouns }}, watch it, and get paid within a minute. Every race is a real race video, and the result is set by the footage, not by the odds. It's play money only.

Tap the two stripes next to the logo to open the lobby and switch between games. Your balance and bet history carry over.

A round, step by step

  1. Pick your bets. Tap a price on the race card to add it to the betslip.
  2. Set your stake on the betslip and tap Place bet. The stake comes off your balance straight away.
  3. Watch the race. The running order and your bets update live. Tap Skip to results to jump to the finish.
  4. See the result. Winnings are added to your balance. Then pick Next race for a new field, or Rebet to place the same bets again.

Odds and returns

  • The odds are fixed when you add a bet. A winning bet returns stake × odds, which includes your stake.
  • On bets that cover several combinations, the stake is per combination. The betslip shows the total cost and the maximum return.
  • Each race has a new field and new odds. The favourite changes from race to race.

During the race

Under Your bets, each bet shows Winning if it would pay out if the race ended now, In reach if a small change in the order would make it pay, or Behind. Winning bets are listed first.
